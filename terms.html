Ministry Messenger Terms of Use

Last updated: October 27, 2026

By using Ministry Messenger you agree to these terms. You accept them in the app before your account is created.

Zero tolerance

Ministry Messenger has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. You agree not to send content that is abusive, threatening, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit, exploitative, fraudulent, or otherwise objectionable, and not to impersonate others.

Reporting and blocking

You can report any conversation and block any account at any time from inside the conversation. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Content that violates these terms is removed, and accounts that post it are blocked from Ministry Messenger.

Your account

Your Ministry Messenger account is connected to your iCloud account. You can delete your account at any time from My Profile in the app.

Group Chats and Announcements

In a Group Chat, every member sees every message and reply. The person who started a Group Chat can add and remove members. An Announcement is sent to each person separately, and replies go only to the sender.
