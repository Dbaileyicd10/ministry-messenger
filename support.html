Ministry Messenger Support

Private messages, Group Chats, Announcements, and multi-day Ministry Series for ministry leaders and the people they serve.

Contact us

Email generationsarising@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. For a safety concern about a message, use Report Conversation in the conversation; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. For an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Getting started

What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad signed in to iCloud. Your Messenger account uses your iCloud account, so there's no separate password.

How do I connect with someone?
Open My Profile and share your invitation link. When the other person taps it in Ministry Messenger, you are connected and appear in each other's People list. You can also share your Messenger Code.

Kinds of messages

People: a private conversation with one person.

Group Chat: everyone in the group sees every message and every reply. To see who is in a Group Chat, open it and tap ⋯ → Group Members. The person who started the group can tap Add People or Remove there. Anyone can rename the group or leave it.

Announcement: your message goes to each person separately. When someone replies, only you see it. Make an Announcement Group once (for example "Prayer Team") and reuse it. Swipe left on an Announcement Group to change its people or delete it.

Ministry Series: a teaching that runs over several days, such as a 21-day prayer guide. Write each day with text, pictures, audio, or links, choose the people or groups, and pick a schedule. On each day the app reminds you that the next day is ready, and one tap sends it.

Siri

Say "Check Ministry Messenger" to hear new messages, "Reply in Ministry Messenger" to answer someone, or "Create a message in Ministry Messenger." You can say a person's full saved name or just their first name. Siri reads back the name and your message before sending.

Safety

Block: in a conversation, tap the menu and choose Block Sender. That account can no longer message you. You can unblock from Blocked Senders in the menu.

Report: in a conversation, tap the menu and choose Report Conversation. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Sensitive photos: photos that may contain nudity are blurred until you choose to view them.

Common questions

Do my lists show up on my other devices?
Yes. Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Ministry Messenger on each iPhone or iPad. Your People, Group Chats, Announcement Groups, and Ministry Series sync through iCloud.

I'm not getting notifications.
Open Settings → Notifications → Ministry Messenger and turn on Allow Notifications. Make sure the device is signed in to iCloud.

I was removed from a Group Chat.
The person who started the group can remove members. You won't receive new messages from that group. Messages you already received stay on your device.

How do I delete my account?
Open My Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your name and Messenger Code, the messages you have sent, and your lists.

Do I need Life Focus?
No. Ministry Messenger works on its own. If you use our Life Focus app, you can send a sermon, prayer, or other entry from Life Focus straight into Ministry Messenger.
