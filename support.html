Ministry Messenger Support

Private messages, Group Chats, Announcements, and multi-day Ministry Series for ministry leaders and the people they serve.

Contact us

Email generationsarising@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. For a safety concern about a message, use Report Conversation in the conversation; reports are reviewed within 24 hours. For an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Getting started

What do I need?
An iPhone or iPad signed in to iCloud. Your Messenger account uses your iCloud account, so there's no separate password.

How do I connect with someone?
Open My Profile and share your invitation link. When the other person taps it in Ministry Messenger, you are connected and appear in each other's People list. You can also share your Messenger Code.

Kinds of messages

People: a private conversation with one person.

Group Chat: everyone in the group sees every message and every reply. To see who is in a Group Chat, open it and tap ⋯ → Group Members. The person who started the group can tap Add People or Remove there. Anyone can rename the group or leave it.

Announcement: your message goes to each person separately. When someone replies, only you see it. Make an Announcement Group once (for example "Prayer Team") and reuse it. Swipe left on an Announcement Group to change its people or delete it.

Ministry Series: a teaching that runs over several days, such as a 21-day prayer guide. Write each day with text, pictures, audio, or links, choose the people or groups, and pick a schedule. On each day the app reminds you that the next day is ready, and one tap sends it.

Siri

Say "Check Ministry Messenger" to hear new messages, "Reply in Ministry Messenger" to answer someone, or "Create a message in Ministry Messenger." You can say a person's full saved name or just their first name. Siri reads back the name and your message before sending.

Safety

Block: in a conversation, tap the menu and choose Block Sender. That account can no longer message you. You can unblock from Blocked Senders in the menu.

Report: in a conversation, tap the menu and choose Report Conversation. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Sensitive photos: photos that may contain nudity are blurred until you choose to view them.

How to use Ministry Messenger

How do I connect with someone?
Tap the pencil (New Message), choose People, then tap +. Tap Invite a Person and send the invitation link by text or email. When they tap the link, you appear in each other's People list.

What's the difference between a Group Chat and an Announcement?
In a Group Chat, everyone sees every message and every reply. An Announcement goes to each person privately, and their replies come back only to you. Use Announcements for prayer partners, volunteers, or anyone you want to reach one-to-one.

How do I send an Announcement?
Your Announcement Groups are at the top of the Messages list. Tap one, type your message, and tap Send. To make a new one, tap the pencil, choose Announcement, then tap +. Press and hold a group to edit its name, people, or photo.

How do I start a Group Chat or add people to one?
Tap the pencil, choose Group Chat, then tap +, name the group, and pick the people. Inside the group, tap the menu and choose Group Members to see everyone. The person who started the group can add or remove people and set the group photo.

How do I send a Ministry Series?
Open the menu (☰) and choose Ministry Series. Create a series, add each day's text, pictures, audio, or links, then tap Start Series and choose who receives it. Send each day by hand, or set a schedule and the app reminds you when the next day is ready.

How do I copy, share, or pass along a message?
Press and hold the message. Your reactions are at the top. Below them, choose Copy, Share, or Forward to send it to someone else in Ministry Messenger. Links in messages can be tapped to open them.

How do I use saved text (Templates)?
While writing a message, tap Templates. Tap + to save text you send often, then tap a template to drop it into your message. Templates sync to your other devices.

How do I add my photo?
Open the menu (☰), choose My Profile, and tap Add Photo. Drag and pinch the picture to fit the circle, then tap Choose. People you message will see it.

Can I use Siri?
Yes. Say "Check Ministry Messenger" to hear new messages, "Reply in Ministry Messenger" to answer someone, "Create a message in Ministry Messenger" to start a new one, or "Send an announcement in Ministry Messenger." In the Shortcuts app you can also build your own, such as a morning prayer message to an Announcement Group.

How do I remove someone from my People list?
Tap the pencil, choose People, tap ⋯ next to the person, and choose Remove from People. Your past messages stay. You can add them again with a new invitation.

How do I report or block someone?
In a conversation, open the menu and choose Report Conversation. In a Group Chat, open Group Members and tap ⋯ next to the person to report or block them. Unblock anyone from the menu (☰) under Blocked Senders.

Will my lists be on my iPad too?
Yes. Use the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Ministry Messenger on each iPhone and iPad. Messages, People, groups, Ministry Series, Templates, and which messages you've read all sync through iCloud.

I'm not getting notifications.
Open Settings, then Notifications, then Ministry Messenger, and turn on Allow Notifications and Badges. Notifications never show who wrote or what they said; open the app to read it.

Common questions

I was removed from a Group Chat.
The person who started the group can remove members. You won't receive new messages from that group. Messages you already received stay on your device.

How do I delete my account?
Open My Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account, your name and Messenger Code, the messages you have sent, and your lists.

Do I need Life Focus?
No. Ministry Messenger works on its own. If you use our Life Focus app, you can send a sermon, prayer, or other entry from Life Focus straight into Ministry Messenger.
