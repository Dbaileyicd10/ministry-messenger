Ministry Messenger Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Ministry Messenger is made by Generations Arising. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and who can see it.

The short version

Ministry Messenger uses Apple's iCloud to deliver your messages. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code. We do not sell or share your information, and we do not read your messages.

Your Messenger account

Your account is connected to the Apple Account you use for iCloud. You don't create a password. When you set up the app, you choose the name other people see, and the app gives you a permanent Messenger Code. Your name and Messenger Code are stored in the app's shared iCloud database so that people you invite can connect with you and send you messages.

If you choose to add a profile photo in My Profile, it is stored with your name in the app's shared iCloud database, and people who can message you can see it. You can change or remove it at any time. Photos are checked on your device for sensitive content before they are used.

People connect only by an invitation link or by your Messenger Code. The app does not search by phone number or email, and it does not read your iPhone contacts.

Messages, photos, and voice messages

When you send a message, the text and any photos or voice recordings are stored in the app's iCloud database, addressed to the people you sent them to. Only your app and their apps request those messages. Group Chat names and member lists are stored the same way so every member's app can show them.

Messages are protected by Apple's iCloud security while they are stored and sent. They are not end-to-end encrypted. As the developer, we do not look at messages. Reports (see below) do not contain any message content.

Information kept in your private iCloud

These items are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only your devices can read:

  • your list of Messenger People, Group Chats, and Announcement Groups
  • which conversations you have hidden or left
  • your Ministry Series, including their days, pictures, audio, and links
  • an email or phone number, only if you entered one in an earlier version of the app (it is never shown to others)

Your block list is stored in iCloud so that blocked accounts can no longer send you messages.

Information kept only on your device

Text templates, which messages you have read, and your agreement to the Terms of Use are kept on your device.

Safety features

If a received photo may contain nudity, the app can blur it using Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis. This check happens entirely on your device; the photo is not sent anywhere to be checked. Outgoing messages are also checked on your device for objectionable words before they are sent.

When you report a conversation, the app saves only the reported account's Messenger Code, the reason you chose, and the date. It does not include your identity, names, or any message content. When you block someone, they can no longer send you messages.

Notifications and Siri

New-message notifications are delivered by Apple through iCloud. They only say that you have a new Ministry Messenger message; they do not show who sent it or what it says. If you use Siri to read or send messages, Siri is provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app gives Siri the names of your Messenger People on your device so it can understand them.

Life Focus

If you also use our Life Focus app, you can choose to send text from Life Focus into Ministry Messenger, and link a Life Focus person to one of your Messenger People. That link is stored on your device.

Deleting your information

In the app, open My Profile and tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your Messenger account, your name, profile photo and Messenger Code, the messages you have sent, your People and Group lists, and your block list. You can also delete a single conversation from your Messages list, or leave a Group Chat at any time.

Children

Ministry Messenger is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Apple and iCloud

Apple stores iCloud data for the app. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple protects it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
